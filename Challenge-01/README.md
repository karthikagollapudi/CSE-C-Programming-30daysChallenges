##Aim

To design a C program to calculate the sum, average, and percentage of marks scored by a student in five subjects.

##Algorithm

Start

Declare five variables that will hold the marks of all the five subjects.

Also declare the variables that will hold the total, average, and percentage.

Read the five subjects' marks from the user.

Sum up all the five marks and store it in total.

Divide the total by 5 to get the average.

To get the percentage, divide the total by 500 and multiply the result by 100.

Display total, average, and percentage.

Stop

##Formula

Total = M1 + M2 + M3 + M4 + M5

Average = Total / 5

Percentage = (Total / 500) × 100
