# Challenge 03 – SMARTCART

## Problem Statement
SMARTCART is a C program that calculates customer discounts, delivery charges, and the final payable amount based on the purchase amount, customer type, and delivery distance.

## Algorithm
1. Start.
2. 
3. Read the purchase amount, customer type, and delivery distance.
4. 
5. Validate the amount and distance.
6. 
7. Calculate the discount based on customer type and purchase amount.

9. Calculate the amount after discount.
10. 
11. Determine the delivery charge based on distance.
12. 
13. Calculate the final payable amount.
14. 
15. Display the bill.
16. 
17. Stop.

## Formulas
- Amount After Discount = Purchase Amount - Discount
- Final Payable Amount = Amount After Discount + Delivery Charge
